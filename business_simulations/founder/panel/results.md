## What would flip a no

- Verifiable open-source client code with proven zero-knowledge encryption and independent clinical validation.
- A usable free tier for late-night venting or a student discount around $3 a month.
- A blended option that includes real therapist supervision or verified clinical backing showing outcomes equal to human care.
- A solid free tier with a student plan under $3 a month, or proving it's actually deeper and more interactive than standard chatbots.
- Proof of clinically verified stress reduction or a hybrid model connected to real human therapists.
- Concrete proof or demonstrable examples during onboarding showing nuanced, clinician-backed DBT exercises rather than generic chatbot responses.
- Clinical validation and proof that the DBT tools provide meaningful, non-hallucinatory guidance rather than generic chatbot answers.
- Proof that the DBT exercises actively de-escalate workday panic attacks faster and better than custom ChatGPT prompts.
- A solid free tier for late-night grounding or student pricing around $2-$3 a month with proof that DBT exercises help way more than general chat.
- A completely free, no-credit-card trial for two full weeks so I can prove to myself I actually use it past day four.
- A verified third-party security audit or fully offline, open-source proof that my logs never leave my device.
- Clear clinical backing showing it genuinely helps bridge waitlists until a human therapist is available.
